Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Half It" mobile game. The Application is provided as a Free service and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or phone number. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob

2. Advertising Identifiers

This application uses Advertising Identifiers (AAID/IDFA) provided by your mobile device. These identifiers are used by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to serve personalized advertisements and analyze ad performance. You can reset these identifiers in your device settings.

3. Game Data & Local Storage

Your progress, such as high scores and unlocked levels, is stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete it from our local records.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact us via the developer contact information on the Google Play Store.
